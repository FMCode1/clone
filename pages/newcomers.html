Welcome to RCCG House of Peace

We are thrilled to have you! Whether you’re worshipping for the first time or exploring your faith, we want to make you feel at home.

What to Expect

  • Warm and friendly welcome from our church family
  • Engaging worship and uplifting sermons
  • Opportunities to connect with different ministries
  • Guidance for children, youth, and adults
  • Information on community events and programs
  • NOTE: Please do not be in a hurry to leave, we have a special package for you at the end of service.

A Place for Everybody

child_care

Children

The Children’s Ministry lovingly guides our little ones in the ways of the Lord. Through Bible teachings, songs, and interactive activities, they are raised in a joyful and nurturing environment where they begin to know and love God from an early age.

groups

Youths

The Youth Ministry is a vibrant community where young people grow in faith and purpose. Through fellowship, mentorship, and impactful programs, they are encouraged to stand firm in Christ and live boldly as lights in their generation.

emoji_events

Excellent Men

The Excellent Men’s Ministry is committed to raising strong and godly men. Through fellowship, prayer, and the Word, men are strengthened to lead with integrity, love their families, and reflect Christ in every area of their lives.

female

Good Women

The Good Women’s Ministry is a place of strength, grace, and encouragement. Here, women are built up through prayer, fellowship, and the Word to live purposefully, support one another, and shine as virtuous examples in their homes and communities.

elderly

Elders

The Elders are pillars of wisdom and spiritual guidance in the church. With hearts devoted to God and years of faithful service, they provide counsel, support, and leadership, helping to uphold the vision and strengthen the body of Christ.
