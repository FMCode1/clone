Giving

Giving is an act of worship and a way to express our gratitude to God for His goodness and faithfulness. Your offerings support the work of the ministry and help us continue to impact lives within our church and community.

Ways to Give

Interac e-Transfer:
You can give securely by sending your offering via Interac e-Transfer to our official church email: rccghop@yahoo.com.
And for the Church Building Project: rccghofpeace@gmail.com.
Please include your full name and, if applicable, the purpose of your giving (tithe, offering, building project, etc.) in the message section.

In-Person Giving:
Offerings can also be given during our church services. Envelopes are available, and our ushers will guide you during the offering time.

Important Notice:
Please note that we do not process any payments or financial transactions through this website. All giving is done only through the official channels listed above.
